# Old vs New Tax Regime Guide (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)

## Overview
Under the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961, individual salaried taxpayers can choose between two tax regimes: the **Old Tax Regime** and the **New Tax Regime** (under Section 115BAC).

As per Budget 2023 and amendments in Budget 2024, the **New Tax Regime is the default regime** for all individual taxpayers. If a taxpayer wishes to calculate tax under the Old Regime, they must explicitly opt for it while filing their return under Form 10-IEA (or via their employer declaration).

---

## Key Differences

| Parameter | Old Tax Regime | New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) |
|---|---|---|
| **Default Status** | Optional | **Default Regime** |
| **Standard Deduction (Salaried)** | ₹50,000 | **₹75,000** (raised in Budget 2024) |
| **Section 87A Full Rebate Limit** | Taxable Income up to ₹5,00,000 (Max ₹12,500) | **Taxable Income up to ₹7,00,000** (Max ₹25,000) |
| **Zero Tax Salaried Threshold** | Gross Income up to ₹5.5 Lakhs | **Gross Income up to ₹7.75 Lakhs** (₹7L + ₹75k std ded) |
| **Major Chapter VI-A Deductions** | Allowed (80C, 80D, 80CCD, etc.) | **Not Allowed** |
| **HRA & LTA Exemptions** | Allowed | **Not Allowed** |
| **Home Loan Interest (Self-occupied 24b)** | Up to ₹2,00,000 | **Not Allowed** |
| **NPS Employer Contribution 80CCD(2)** | Allowed up to 10% of salary | **Allowed up to 14% of salary** |
| **Tax Slabs Complexity** | 4 slabs (0%, 5%, 20%, 30%) | 6 progressive slabs (0%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 30%) |

---

## Tax Slab Comparison (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)

### New Tax Regime Slabs (Salaried)
- **Up to ₹3,00,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000:** 5%
- **₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** 10%
- **₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000:** 15%
- **₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹15,00,000:** 30%

### Old Tax Regime Slabs
- **Up to ₹2,50,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000:** 5%
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000:** 30%

*Note: In both regimes, a Health & Education Cess of 4% is levied on total income tax.*

---

## Decision Rule: Which Regime Should You Pick?
- **Choose New Regime if:** Your total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + Home Loan Interest) are relatively low (typically less than ₹3.75 Lakhs to ₹4.25 Lakhs depending on income level). It offers lower slab rates and eliminates paperwork.
- **Choose Old Regime if:** You pay significant home loan interest (up to ₹2L under Section 24b), pay substantial house rent (HRA), maximize 80C (₹1.5L), 80CCD(1B) NPS (₹50k), and family health insurance (₹25k–₹50k).
