# Guide to Income Tax Return (ITR) Forms for Salaried Individuals

## Overview
Selecting the correct ITR form is essential. Filing an incorrect form renders the return defective under Section 139(9) of the Income Tax Act.

---

## ITR-1 (Sahaj)
### Who Can File ITR-1?
Resident individuals having total income up to **₹50 Lakhs** during the financial year, where income originates from:
1. **Salary or Pension**.
2. **One House Property** (excluding cases where loss is brought forward from previous years).
3. **Other Sources** (interest on savings, fixed deposits, family pension, dividend income).
4. **Agricultural Income** up to ₹5,000.

### Who CANNOT File ITR-1?
- Total income exceeds ₹50,00,000.
- Non-Residents (NRI) or Resident Not Ordinarily Resident (RNOR).
- Individuals having income from capital gains (sale of shares, mutual funds, property).
- Individuals owning more than one house property.
- Directors in an Indian company or holding unlisted equity shares.
- Individuals with foreign assets, foreign income, or signing authority in foreign accounts.
- Income from business or profession.

---

## ITR-2
### Who Should File ITR-2?
Individuals and Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs) not having income from "Profits and Gains of Business or Profession":
1. Salaried individuals with income exceeding **₹50 Lakhs**.
2. Salaried individuals who earned **Capital Gains** (short-term or long-term) from stock market trading, mutual funds redemptions, cryptocurrency, or property sale.
3. Individuals owning **more than one house property**.
4. Individuals holding **unlisted equity shares** or directorship in companies.
5. Individuals with **foreign assets** (e.g. US stocks like RSUs/ESPPs from employer) or foreign income.
6. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) with salary or property income in India.

---

## ITR-3 & ITR-4
- **ITR-3:** For individuals having income from business, freelance profession, or intraday/F&O derivatives trading.
- **ITR-4 (Sugam):** For resident individuals with presumptive income under Section 44AD, 44ADA, or 44AE up to ₹50 Lakhs.
