# Deductions & Exemptions for Indian Salaried Taxpayers

## Overview
Deductions under Chapter VI-A and exemptions under Section 10 are the primary methods for salaried individuals to reduce their gross taxable income under the **Old Tax Regime**.

---

## 1. Standard Deduction
- **Section:** 16(ia)
- **Eligibility:** Available to all salaried individuals and pensioners automatically without producing bills or investment proofs.
- **Amounts for FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26):**
  - **New Tax Regime:** **₹75,000**
  - **Old Tax Regime:** **₹50,000**

---

## 2. Section 80C (Investments & Expenditures)
- **Maximum Ceiling:** **₹1,50,000 per financial year**.
- **Eligible Instruments:**
  - Employee Provident Fund (EPF) and Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF)
  - Public Provident Fund (PPF)
  - Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS mutual funds - 3-year lock-in)
  - National Savings Certificate (NSC)
  - Tax-saving 5-year Fixed Deposits
  - Life Insurance Premium (LIC / term insurance)
  - Principal repayment of Home Loan
  - Tuition fees for up to 2 children
  - Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
- **Regime Applicability:** Old Regime only.

---

## 3. Section 80D (Health Insurance Premium & Preventive Health Checkups)
- **Scope:** Medical insurance premiums paid for self, spouse, children, and parents.
- **Deduction Limits:**
  - **Self, Spouse & Dependent Children (< 60 yrs):** Up to **₹25,000**.
  - **Self/Spouse (Senior Citizens >= 60 yrs):** Up to **₹50,000**.
  - **Parents (< 60 yrs):** Additional deduction up to **₹25,000**.
  - **Parents (Senior Citizens >= 60 yrs):** Additional deduction up to **₹50,000**.
  - **Preventive Health Checkup:** Up to **₹5,000** within the overall sub-limits mentioned above.
- **Maximum Potential Benefit:** Up to **₹1,00,000** if both taxpayer and parents are senior citizens (₹50,000 + ₹50,000).
- **Payment Mode:** Must be non-cash (UPI, card, net banking), except preventive health checkup which can be paid in cash.

---

## 4. House Rent Allowance (HRA) Exemption
- **Section:** 10(13A) read with Rule 2A.
- **Condition:** Employee must be receiving HRA as part of salary structure and living in a rented house paying rent.
- **Exemption Amount:** Lowest of the following three:
  1. Actual HRA received from employer.
  2. 50% of Basic Salary + DA (for metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) OR 40% of Basic Salary + DA (for non-metro cities).
  3. Actual Rent Paid minus 10% of Basic Salary + DA.
- **Landlord PAN Requirement:** Mandatory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000.
- **Regime Applicability:** Old Regime only.

---

## 5. Section 80CCD(1B) - Additional NPS Deduction
- **Amount:** Up to **₹50,000** over and above the ₹1,50,000 cap of Section 80C.
- **Total Combined 80C + 80CCD(1B):** Up to **₹2,00,000**.
- **Regime Applicability:** Old Regime only.

---

## 6. Section 24(b) - Home Loan Interest
- **Self-Occupied Property:** Deduction of interest paid up to **₹2,00,000** per financial year.
- **Let-Out Property:** Full interest deductible against rental income, though net loss from house property eligible for set-off against salary is capped at ₹2,00,000 per year.
- **Regime Applicability:** Old Regime only.
