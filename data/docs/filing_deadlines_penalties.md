# Income Tax Filing Deadlines, Belated Returns & Penalties

## 1. Important Due Dates for Salaried Individuals (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)

| Milestone | Due Date | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Original Filing Deadline** | **July 31, 2025** | Deadline for individuals not liable to tax audit (salaried employees). |
| **Belated Return Deadline** | **December 31, 2025** | Last date to file a missed return under Section 139(4). |
| **Revised Return Deadline** | **December 31, 2025** | Last date to correct mistakes in filed return under Section 139(5). |
| **Updated Return (ITR-U)** | Up to **24 months** from end of relevant AY | To declare undisclosed income under Section 139(8A) with additional tax. |

---

## 2. Consequences of Missing the July 31 Deadline

### Late Filing Fee (Section 234F)
If return is filed after July 31 but on or before December 31:
- **Total Income up to ₹5,00,000:** Late fee is capped at **₹1,000**.
- **Total Income exceeding ₹5,00,000:** Late fee is **₹5,000**.
- If total income is below the basic exemption threshold, no penalty is levied under Section 234F.

### Interest on Outstanding Tax
- **Section 234A:** Simple interest @ **1% per month** (or fraction of a month) on unpaid tax liability from August 1 until the date of filing.
- **Section 234B & 234C:** Interest for default or deferment in payment of advance tax (applicable if net unpaid tax exceeds ₹10,000).

### Loss of Carry Forward Privileges
If return is filed after July 31:
- Losses under capital gains (STCL, LTCL) or business losses **cannot be carried forward** to future financial years to offset profits.
- *Exception:* Loss under house property can still be carried forward even with a belated return.

## 3. Step-by-Step Filing Process on Income Tax Portal
1. **Gather Documents:** Form 16 (Part A & Part B from employer), AIS (Annual Information Statement), TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary), Form 26AS, bank interest certificates.
2. **Reconciliation:** Match TDS deducted in Form 16 with Form 26AS and AIS on `https://eportal.incometax.gov.in`.
3. **Portal Login & Prefill:** Log in using PAN as User ID. Select Assessment Year (AY 2025-26 for FY 2024-25), Online mode, and appropriate form (ITR-1 or ITR-2).
4. **Regime Confirmation:** Review pre-filled income; decide between default New Regime or opt out to Old Regime.
5. **Tax Computation & Verification:** Pay any self-assessment tax (Challan 280) if liability exists.
6. **E-Verification (Critical Step):** Return must be e-verified within **30 days** of filing via Aadhaar OTP, Net Banking, or Demat EVC. An unverified return is legally deemed invalid.
